The tour

Five places. One calm system.

Studio for layouts, Widgets for everything live, Browsers for sessions that stay, Appearance for the look, and Settings for the rest.

Studio. Pick a display, start from a layout, then drag, resize and split tiles on a live board. Widgets render for real while you arrange them.

Widgets

Sixteen widgets. One family.

Every widget takes its color from your accent and its shape from its category, so a wall of them still feels like one piece. Set each up once; it works in every workspace.

  • Daily Clock, Calendar, Weather, Ruter, Notes
  • Connected Spotify, Discord, Inbox, News, Reddit
  • Device System, Battery, Volume, AI Usage, Bambu Lab
  • Play Scrapbots
Shared browsers

Fullscreen, without losing the rest of your desk.

Put a named browser in any tile. Go fullscreen on a video and it fills the tile, not the monitor, so your widgets, chat and editor stay right where they are.

  • tab
    Named sessions that stay. Media, Research, Music: each keeps its own tabs, logins and playback, across every workspace.
  • fullscreen
    Fullscreen inside the tile. One switch per tile. Video players, slides and games expand to the tile's edges and stop there.
  • bookmarks
    A toolbar that gets out of the way. Tabs, address and your Brave bookmarks, or collapse it to a single slim row.
  • shield
    Private by design. Web pages get no access to Setpiece, and uBlock Origin Lite ships built in.
Appearance

One accent colors everything.

Pick an accent, or any color you like. Setpiece builds a full Material 3 palette from it, in light and dark, and every page, control and widget follows along.

Also on the Appearance page: surface opacity, wallpaper dimming, accent glow, corner roundness, and eleven wallpapers, some of them moving.

And the rest

Built around the apps you already use.

monitor

Every display, its own layout

Include the displays a workspace uses, portrait ones too, and give each its own board at its real aspect ratio.

drag_pan

Drag, resize, snap

Move tiles freely or with smart snap. Drop one tile on another to swap them. Undo is always one Ctrl+Z away.

dashboard_customize

Start from a layout

Columns, rows, grid or main-and-side. Preview first, pick the tile count, then apply. Tile contents keep their order.

swap_horiz

Workspaces for every mode

Deep focus, streaming, a Friday of meetings. Switch in one click; unsaved changes are never lost silently.

restart_alt

Put it all back

Stop the workspace, or just close Setpiece, and your windows and taskbars return to where they were.

lock

Local and encrypted

Workspaces live on your PC. Connection secrets are encrypted for your Windows account and never leave it.

Get Setpiece

Build and run on Windows 11.

The repository provides the source. A prebuilt download isn't published yet.

PowerShell · repository root
cd UI
pnpm install --frozen-lockfile
cd ..
.\build.ps1

Then run Setpiece.exe from Setpiece/bin/Release/net10.0-windows/.
